# FAME v1.1.0 — Companion manuscripts and post-deployment monitoring

This release extends the FAME reproducibility archive from a single temporal
transfer study into two explicitly separated companion research tracks.

## Companion track 1 — FAME/DOC temporal transferability

Adds explicit documentation and reproducibility organization for analyses of:

- Decision-Oriented Calibration (DOC);
- temporal transfer of learned prediction-to-decision representations;
- calibration stability;
- fantasy-football multi-season transfer;
- Energy expanding-window transfer;
- predictive-versus-operational transfer.

## Companion track 2 — DRR/DRVM post-deployment monitoring

Adds:

- Decision-Representation Regret (DRR) validation materials;
- Decision-Representation Validity Monitor (DRVM) Monte Carlo experiments;
- null audit and finite-horizon power/delay outputs;
- monitorability calculations;
- fantasy-football and Energy historical deployment replays;
- temporal reference-risk stress tests;
- baseline-uplift sensitivity;
- post-alert representation-renewal computational support.

## Reproducibility organization

New documentation separates the two manuscripts' primary claims and maps them
to distinct computational artifacts.

## Backward compatibility

The v1.0.2 transfer-study archive remains scientifically valid. v1.1.0 adds
new monitoring materials and a clearer companion-manuscript organization; it
does not overwrite or reinterpret the archived v1.0.2 outputs.
