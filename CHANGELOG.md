- explicit separation of the project into two companion research tracks:
  - FAME/DOC temporal transferability;
  - DRR/DRVM post-deployment validity monitoring;
- `docs/MANUSCRIPTS.md` describing manuscript scope and non-overlap;
- `docs/REPRODUCIBILITY_MAP.md` mapping claims to computational artifacts;
- DRVM Monte Carlo validation notebook and machine-readable outputs;
- real fantasy-football and Energy DRVM replay notebook and outputs;

  post-deployment monitoring as distinct empirical protocols.

- restrictions on redistribution of third-party raw data;
- existing repository license.
