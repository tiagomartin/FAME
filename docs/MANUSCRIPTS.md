# Companion manuscripts

The FAME repository now supports two companion manuscripts with deliberately
non-overlapping primary scientific questions.

## 1. FAME/DOC: temporal transferability

**Working title:**
*Temporal Transferability of Learned Prediction-to-Decision Representations in
Predictive-Prescriptive Systems*

**Target journal:** TOP — Transactions in Operations Research

**Primary question:**
Do historically calibrated prediction-to-decision representations transfer
operational value to future periods?

**Exclusive contribution set**

- FAME modular prediction-to-decision architecture;
- Decision-Oriented Calibration (DOC);
- calibration-to-evaluation temporal transfer;
- calibration stability;
- retrospective displacement of favorable representation regions;
- predictive-versus-operational transfer;
- fantasy-football and Energy transfer experiments.

**Not part of this manuscript**

- Decision-Representation Regret (DRR);
- Decision-Representation Validity Monitor (DRVM);
- anytime-valid false-signal control;
- monitorability theory;
- baseline-uplift monitoring;
- post-alert renewal.

## 2. DRR/DRVM: post-deployment monitoring

**Working title:**
*Continuous Validity Monitoring of Frozen Prediction-to-Decision
Representations for Constrained Optimization*

**Target journal:** Annals of Operations Research

**Primary question:**
When should a frozen prediction-to-decision representation be reviewed after
deployment?

**Exclusive contribution set**

- Decision-Representation Regret (DRR);
- predictive-validity / representation-validity non-equivalence;
- anytime-valid Decision-Representation Validity Monitor (DRVM);
- binary-KL reference construction;
- contextual reference thresholds;
- representation separability;
- first-order monitorability;
- O(J) online DRVM recursion;
- temporal reference-risk stress tests;
- baseline-uplift sensitivity;
- post-alert representation renewal.

**Not part of this manuscript**

- the complete fantasy-football transfer table;
- the three DOC temporal-transfer experiments;
- DOC bootstrap recovery series;
- retrospective future-optimum displacement as a transfer result;
- the nine Energy transfer experiments;
- predictive-versus-operational transfer figures and summaries.

## Shared application domains

Fantasy football and Energy appear in both manuscripts for different purposes.

- In the FAME/DOC manuscript, the domains test **temporal transferability**.
- In the DRVM manuscript, the domains are **historical deployment replays**
  used to test post-freeze monitoring.

The chronologies and frozen representations are not identical across the two
empirical designs, and results should not be interpreted as duplicated analyses.

## Status metadata

The repository should describe both works as **companion manuscripts** until
journal-specific bibliographic metadata (volume, pages, DOI) become available.
Do not label either manuscript as accepted or published before that occurs.
